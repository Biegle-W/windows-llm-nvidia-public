Archived launchers retain historical runtime assumptions; do not run them from
this directory. A deliberate restoration requires matching hardware, dependencies,
original layout and fresh resource/quality validation. Private inactive binaries
and backups remain ignored under the separate `archive/` directory. They are not
part of this tracked source archive. No weights, chats, settings or runtimes were
moved or deleted by repository reorganization.

Dated raw benchmark/verification payloads remain in their recorded locations;
older speeds are not current RTX 3060 / 16 GB performance claims. Maintain Windows
archives independently from Ubuntu's former dual-NVIDIA archive.
