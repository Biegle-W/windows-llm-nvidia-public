# Installed RTX 3080 Ti versus RTX 3060 — October 25, 2026

The installed RTX 3080 Ti passes CUDA, short chat, streaming and two generation
samples for all four maintained models. The largest gain is LFM2.5 at 78.35
tokens/s, 20.0% above the saved unlocked RTX 3060 mean. Dense Qwen27B is slightly
slower with the current automatic CPU/GPU placement.

The current fit leaves approximately150–205MiB more weights in RAM. Qwen27B has
two fewer GPU layers, which confounds its small regression. Layer counts alone
do not mean all weights reside in VRAM, including for LFM. CPU weights and CPU
attention limit how much a faster GPU can improve these maximum-context profiles.
No kernel profiler or matched-residency experiment was performed. Background
memory use, clocks and thermals were not held constant across sessions.
